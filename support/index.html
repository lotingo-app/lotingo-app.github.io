Frequently asked questions

Does Lotingo need an internet connection?

No. Lotingo requires no internet connection, and everything from calling the numbers to checking a Bingo runs on the phone itself. The app only goes online to load ads and to send crash reports and usage statistics, and it simply skips those when there is no connection.

The app closed in the middle of a round. Is the round lost?

No. Open Lotingo again and the round carries on from exactly where it stopped, with every number that was already drawn.

I tapped a link, the app opened and asked me to update. Why?

That link was made for a newer version of Lotingo than the one on your phone. Update the app from Google Play or the App Store, then tap the link again.

How do I change my privacy choices?

The Privacy Policy explains what the app collects and where you can change each choice.
