How bingo is played

The rules fit in three lines, which is why everyone from kids to grandparents can join in.

  1. Everyone gets a card

    Each card holds a grid of numbers. In Lotto 90, the Vietnamese lô tô, every row has five numbers between 1 and 90.

  2. The caller draws the numbers

    Numbers are drawn one at a time and called out to the whole room.

  3. Complete a pattern and shout Bingo

    Players mark every number they have. One number away? You are on One to go. Complete the pattern, or a full row in Lotto, and shout Bingo!
